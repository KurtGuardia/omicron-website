# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

At **OMICRON Consultores SRL**, we value the privacy of the people who visit our website. This Policy explains, in general terms, what data may be collected, for what purposes and how it may be handled.

## 1. Scope
This Policy applies to the institutional website of OMICRON Consultores SRL and to data that may be collected through forms, email links, contact buttons or basic analytics tools.

## 2. Data we may collect
Depending on how you interact with the site, we may collect:
- contact data you voluntarily submit, such as name, email address, institution, phone number and message;
- basic browsing information, such as pages visited, date and time of access, browser, language, approximate device information and aggregated usage metrics;
- technical information necessary for security, performance and abuse prevention.

## 3. Purposes
Data may be used to:
- respond to inquiries or requests you submit;
- follow up on a business or institutional conversation initiated by the user;
- improve site content, performance and user experience;
- protect site security and prevent misuse.

## 4. Good-faith and minimization principles
Only data reasonably necessary for the purposes described above should be collected. OMICRON acts in good faith and follows principles of minimization, relevance and proportionality.

## 5. Cookies and analytics
The site may use technical cookies or equivalent tools required for site operation and, where applicable, for basic performance measurement or aggregated usage analytics. If additional analytics or marketing tools are incorporated in the future, this Policy should be updated accordingly.

## 6. Data sharing
OMICRON does not sell personal data. Information may only be shared when necessary to:
- provide or manage the requested service;
- technically operate the site through technology providers;
- comply with legal obligations or lawful requests from competent authorities.

## 7. Retention
Data will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain basic institutional traceability, comply with applicable obligations or resolve incidents.

## 8. Security
Reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures will be adopted to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or misuse. However, no internet-based system is completely secure.

## 9. User rights
If you want to request an update, correction, deletion or review of information you submitted directly through the website, you may do so through the official contact channel published on the site.

## 10. External links
The site may contain links to third-party websites. This Policy does not apply to those external sites, so we recommend reviewing their own privacy policies.

## 11. Changes to this Policy
OMICRON may update this Policy when needed. The current version will be the one published on the site together with its update date.

## 12. Contact
For privacy-related questions or data-processing inquiries, please use the official contact channel published on the website.
